Peacock Mantis Shrimp.

Meet the namesake

A brilliant little heavyweight

The peacock mantis shrimp (Odontodactylus scyllarus) wears a remarkable mix of greens, blues and reds. Despite its name, it is a stomatopod, a separate order of crustaceans rather than a true shrimp. [1] It lives in warm Indian and Pacific waters, sheltering among rocks and in seabed burrows. [2]

This species is a smasher: its folded feeding appendages end in clubs that strike hard prey. Researchers used high-speed imaging to show how a spring mechanism stores and releases energy for the blow, which can also create collapsing vapor bubbles beside its target. [3]

Mantis shrimp eyes are complex, but more color receptors do not automatically mean finer color discrimination. Experiments on another species, Haptosquilla trispinosa, found surprisingly poor discrimination between nearby colors. That result calls for care with claims of superior vision. [4]

Its vivid appearance inspired Peacock Mantis Shrimp: Slipped My Mind. The game's colorful code pegs are a playful software metaphor; real mantis shrimp are not known to solve our codebreaking puzzles.
